About MonGoats

MonGoats archives oral stories that would otherwise only live in someone's memory — a grandparent's Mongolian folktale, a Japanese travel memory, a Russian recollection — and turns them into searchable, translated knowledge cards.

The pipeline

Upload audio or video → Chimege (Mongolian) or ElevenLabs (Japanese, Russian) transcribes it → Gemini translates and structures it into a title, summary and key points → it lands in the archive as a story card anyone can swipe through.
